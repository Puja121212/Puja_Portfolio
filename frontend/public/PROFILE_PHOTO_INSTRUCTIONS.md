# Profile Photo Setup Instructions

## How to Add Your Profile Photo:

1. **Choose your photo** (recommended: professional headshot)
2. **Resize to 400x400 pixels** (square format works best)
3. **Save as JPG format** with name: `profile-photo.jpg`
4. **Copy the file** to: `frontend/public/profile-photo.jpg`
